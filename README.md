                        <h5>Core Content</h5>
                        <p><strong>Witchcraft is not magic in the way of fantasy.</strong> It is the practice of directing energy, intention, and will toward manifesting change in the world. It is rooted in deep connection with nature, respect for all living things, and an understanding that energy flows through all existence.</p>

                        <p>Witchcraft encompasses:</p>
                        <ul>
                            <li><strong>Wicca:</strong> A modern spiritual religion honoring the God and Goddess</li>
                            <li><strong>Herbalism:</strong> Working with the healing and magical properties of plants</li>
                            <li><strong>Ritual:</strong> Sacred acts performed with intention and reverence</li>
                            <li><strong>Spellcraft:</strong> Focused work to bring about desired change</li>
                            <li><strong>Divination:</strong> Tools for understanding hidden knowledge (tarot, runes, etc.)</li>
                        </ul>

                        <p><em>"As Brandie learned in Holy Bible 2, witchcraft is not about power over others—it is about understanding and honoring the sacred forces at work in the universe. It is about integrity, accountability, and walking your authentic path."</em></p>

                        <h5>Practical Exercise</h5>
                        <p><strong>Reflection Journal:</strong> Write about what drew you to witchcraft. What do you hope to learn? What are your fears or misconceptions? Keep this journal throughout your studies.</p>

                    <div class="lesson-header">
                        <h4>Lesson 3: Sacred Circle Casting</h4>
                        <span class="lesson-number">3/6</span>
                    </div>
                    
                    <div class="lesson-content">
                        <h5>Learning Objectives</h5>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Understand the purpose of casting a circle</li>
                            <li>Learn the step-by-step process</li>
                            <li>Create a protective sacred space</li>
                            <li>Practice opening and closing circles safely</li>
                        </ul>

                        <h5>Why Cast a Circle?</h5>
                        <p>A sacred circle is a protected space where you can work magic with focused intention. It is a boundary between the material world and the spiritual realms, a container for your energy, and a shield against unwanted influences.</p>

                        <h5>How to Cast a Circle</h5>
                        <ol>
                            <li><strong>Cleanse Your Space:</strong> Use sage, salt water, or sound to clear the area of negative energy</li>
                            <li><strong>Ground Yourself:</strong> Place your hands on the earth (or floor), breathe deeply, feel your connection to the ground</li>
                            <li><strong>Cast the Circle:</strong> Walk clockwise (deosil) around the space. Point your wand, athame, or hand outward. Visualize a circle of white light forming as you walk</li>
                            <li><strong>Call the Elements:</strong> Standing in the center, face each direction and call: "I call upon Fire of the South..." (continue for each element)</li>
                            <li><strong>Invoke Protection:</strong> Speak words of protection: "This circle is sealed. Only love and light may enter. All negativity is banished."</li>
                            <li><strong>Work Your Magic:</strong> Perform rituals, spellwork, or meditation within the circle</li>
                            <li><strong>Close the Circle:</strong> Walk counter-clockwise (widdershins), visualizing the circle of light dissolving. Thank each element. Speak: "The circle is open, but never broken. Merry meet, and merry part, and merry meet again."</li>
                        </ol>

                        <h5>Practical Exercise</h5>
                        <p><strong>Circle Casting Practice:</strong> In your home or outdoors, cast a small circle. Sit within it for 10-15 minutes. Notice how the energy feels. Practice opening and closing the circle. This is a foundational skill you will use throughout your witchcraft journey.</p>

                    <div class="lesson-header">
                        <h4>Lesson 4: Ethical Witchcraft</h4>
                        <span class="lesson-number">4/6</span>
                    </div>
                    
                    <div class="lesson-content">
                        <h5>Learning Objectives</h5>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Understand the Wiccan Rede and the Rule of Three</li>
                            <li>Learn ethical considerations in spellwork</li>
                            <li>Develop integrity in your practice</li>
                            <li>Honor the consequences of your magic</li>
                        </ul>

                        <h5>The Wiccan Rede</h5>
                        <p><em>"An it harm none, do what ye will."</em></p>
                        <p>The primary ethical principle of Wicca. Before any working, ask: "Will this harm anyone?" If the answer is yes, do not proceed. True witchcraft honors free will and causes no harm.</p>

                        <h5>The Rule of Three</h5>
                        <p><em>"Whatever energy you send out into the universe returns to you threefold."</em></p>
                        <p>This is not punishment—it is natural law. Positive energy brings positive returns. Negative energy brings negative consequences. Take responsibility for your magic.</p>

                        <h5>Ethical Considerations</h5>
                        <ul>
                            <li><strong>Consent:</strong> Never work magic on someone without their knowledge or permission</li>
                            <li><strong>Intention:</strong> Be clear about your intent. Vague or mixed intentions create unclear results</li>
                            <li><strong>Accountability:</strong> Own the consequences of your magic. If harm results, take responsibility</li>
                            <li><strong>Truth:</strong> As Brandie teaches us, truth-telling is sacred in witchcraft. Do not use magic to deceive or manipulate</li>
                            <li><strong>Respect Nature:</strong> Honor all living things. Use sustainable practices. Leave no harm in your wake</li>
                        </ul>

                        <p><em>"Brandie's journey teaches us that integrity in witchcraft matters. The spells we cast, the words we speak, and the energy we direct—these shape not just our reality, but the fabric of all existence. Choose carefully. Choose ethically. Choose truth."</em></p>

                        <h5>Practical Exercise</h5>
                        <p><strong>Ethical Reflection:</strong> Write down three things you might want to work magic for. For each one, ask: "Will this harm anyone? Do I have consent? Is my intention clear? Am I willing to accept the consequences?" Only proceed with workings that pass this ethical test.</p>

                <article class="lesson-card">
                    <div class="lesson-header">
                        <h4>Lesson 5: Grounding & Centering</h4>
                        <span class="lesson-number">5/6</span>
                    </div>
                    
                    <div class="lesson-content">
                        <h5>Learning Objectives</h5>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Understand grounding and centering practices</li>
                            <li>Learn techniques to anchor your energy</li>
                            <li>Develop stability and presence</li>
                            <li>Create daily grounding habits</li>
                        </ul>

                        <h5>What is Grounding?</h5>
                        <p>Grounding is connecting your energy to the earth. It anchors you in the present moment and prevents you from becoming scattered or overwhelmed by spiritual energy. Without grounding, you may feel dizzy, unfocused, or disconnected after magical work.</p>

                        <h5>Grounding Techniques</h5>
                        
                        <div class="technique-box">
                            <h6>Physical Grounding</h6>
                            <p>Walk barefoot on earth, grass, or sand. Feel your feet connecting to the ground. Visualize roots growing from the soles of your feet deep into the earth. Draw earth's energy up through your feet, through your body, and back down again in a cycle.</p>
                        </div>

                        <div class="technique-box">
                            <h6>Sensory Grounding</h6>
                            <p>Name 5 things you see, 4 things you feel, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, 1 thing you taste. This brings your awareness fully into your physical body and the present moment.</p>
                        </div>

                        <div class="technique-box">
                            <h6>Breath Grounding</h6>
                            <p>Breathe deeply and slowly. With each inhale, say "I am here." With each exhale, say "I am grounded." Continue for several minutes. Feel your presence solidifying.</p>
                        </div>

                        <div class="technique-box">
                            <h6>Stone Grounding</h6>
                            <p>Hold a stone (obsidian, hematite, or tourmaline are excellent). Feel its weight and solidity. Feel its connection to the earth. Let its grounded energy flow into you.</p>
                        </div>

                        <h5>Centering</h5>
                        <p>Centering is gathering all your scattered energy back into your core. Imagine all your energy—your thoughts, emotions, attention—flowing to your center point (just below your navel). This creates focus and stability for magical work.</p>

                        <h5>Practical Exercise</h5>
                        <p><strong>Daily Grounding Practice:</strong> Each morning, practice one grounding technique for 5-10 minutes. Notice how it affects your day. Which techniques resonate with you? Create a personal grounding ritual that you can use daily, especially before and after magical work.</p>
